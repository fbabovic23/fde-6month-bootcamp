# Industry Consulting Workbooks: Your Briefs (13 Cases)

This is the only workbook file to open before a drill. Each brief tells you what you would know walking into the meeting and nothing more. The full situation, the objection and the grading rubric are in `resources/coach/Workbooks-Coach.md`, which lives in the FDE Client Sparring Partner project. Read a case there only after its last memo has been graded.

## How a case runs

| When | Slot | What you do | Command |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week A · Tuesday | 15 min | Discovery roleplay. Uncover the situation with Mom Test questions. | `Week [X], Run WB[Y] Discovery` |
| Week A · Thursday | open | Catch up or rest. | |
| Week A · Saturday | 25 min | Write a 1-page Problem Brief: problem, who has it, evidence, success metric, out of scope. | `Grade WB[Y] Brief` |
| Week B · Tuesday | open | Catch up or rest. | |
| Week B · Thursday | 15 min | Pushback roleplay. | `Week [X], Run WB[Y] Objection` |
| Week B · Saturday | 25 min | Write a 1-page Risk & Mitigation Memo: data, security, adoption, cost. | `Grade WB[Y] Risks` |

## Briefs

- **WB1 · Healthcare.** You are meeting the operations director of a chain of 12 dental clinics. They have been a customer of your SMS appointment-reminder tool for three months and have asked for a call about results.
- **WB2 · Logistics.** A courier with 400 drivers uses your ETA prediction tool. Your contact in operations says customer complaints are rising.
- **WB3 · Banking.** A regional bank's fraud team says its analysts cannot keep up with the alert queue.
- **WB4 · Retail.** An online fashion retailer is evaluating your AI search against a competitor's.
- **WB5 · Manufacturing.** A plant manager whose factory streams sensor data into your monitoring platform says the night shift keeps reporting a problem.
- **WB6 · Energy.** A utility runs a support chatbot over its policy documents. A customer complaint about it has reached the regulator.
- **WB7 · Insurance.** An insurer wants to pilot your AI for reading claim documents. Their IT security team will be in the meeting.
- **WB8 · Public sector.** A city wants AI to help with a backlog of building permit applications.
- **WB9 · Telecom.** A mobile operator built a churn prediction model six months ago and is unhappy with what it has delivered.
- **WB10 · Airline.** An airline's operations team wants to talk about its crew scheduling app after a bad storm day.
- **WB11 · Pharma.** A pharma company wants your AI to draft sections of clinical trial reports. A security and compliance review comes before any pilot.
- **WB12 · Legal.** A 200-lawyer firm is piloting a contract review agent. You are meeting a partner and the head of IT.
- **WB13 · Media.** A streaming service says, "We want better recommendations." That is the whole brief. This is your final case: run the full FDE loop, like a complete interview.
